QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Does someone who finds my pet need to download an app? +

No! That is the core beauty of MyPet. Anyone with an iPhone or Android phone can simply point their native camera app at the collar tag, and your pet's recovery page opens immediately in their web browser.

Can I hide my phone number and still be reached? +

Yes. You can toggle your phone number and email visibility on or off at any moment in the dashboard. When hidden, finders can still use the "I Found This Pet" form to send you their phone number and GPS location securely.

What happens when I turn on Lost Mode? +

Turning on Lost Mode updates your pet's public page to a prominent missing alert with their last-seen date, time, and location. It also gives you 1-click tools to generate printable 8.5×11 street flyers and social media graphics.

Is MyPet compatible with microchips? +

Absolutely. A microchip requires a special scanner at a vet clinic, whereas a MyPet tag can be read by any neighbor or finder on the street in 3 seconds. They complement each other perfectly to bring pets home faster.
