TRUST & PRIVACY

Built with privacy and pet safety first.

🔒

You control your information

Choose to show or hide your phone number and email. Finders can send secure messages without exposing your personal address.

⚡

Zero friction for finders

Finders don't need to install an app, sign up, or fill out endless forms. A single scan reveals the pet's identity and one-tap call button.

📍

Lost Mode alerts

If your pet wanders off, turn on Lost Mode with one tap. Public profiles switch to high-visibility alert mode with last-seen locations.
